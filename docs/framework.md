# The three-axis framework: theory, stance and surviving conclusions

> Scope: one compressed archive of "a neural network is a signal-processing system": the theoretical frame (A-001 correction included), the author's stance, and the surviving research conclusions of the project close-out (2026-09-25, v0.2 → optimizer v3.0).
> Division of labour: `README.md` is the external entry point (the product line); this document answers **framework-level questions** — whether each of the three axes holds, why, and what may not be claimed. Cross-references to `viewpoint.md` / `research-summary.md` name the documents that this one replaces.

## 0. The original judgment and its 2026-10 correction

Author's judgment (key points of the 2026-09 conversation):

- A neural network is essentially a **signal processing system**: given an input, it maps to an output; learning is essentially **finding a suitable mapping** — matching inputs to suitable outputs.
- Three properties matter most: 1. **information capacity** — how much information the network can hold; 2. **information discriminability** — whether different inputs are distinguishable (the original text: "the essence of training is to make the positions of different inputs in phase space more distinguishable"); 3. **training difficulty** — how to establish the correct input-output mapping.
- These are not three independent accuracy metrics but **three facets of one and the same thing**: capacity decides "whether it can fit", discriminability decides "how neatly it is packed", training difficulty decides "how much cost it takes to pack it in".
- Later qualifier: the goal is not prediction and guidance of network design, but the **task↔network correspondence judgment** — given a task and a network, decide whether they match.

> **2026-10 correction (A-001, by the author himself)**: "phase space" is uniformly corrected to **output space** — training is finding a suitable position in the output space for each different input; the internal state is only a **channel** that carries the input to the output space, and properties are defined only at the output boundary. Not a dispute over wording: it explains in one stroke why axis ② closes (how much can be spread out is by nature a question of information throughput), why axis ③ is not comparable across tasks (the coordinate system of the output space is defined by the task), and why the parameter/representation-space readouts all fail (what they measure is "which set of parameters happened to implement it this time", not the mapping itself — equivalent-solution drift). Only the corrected formulation is used below.

## 1. Verdict at a glance

| Axis | Verdict | Reason in one sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ① Information capacity | **holds** — the only axis that obtained a hard criterion, and the only cross-task one | Fano/DPI give a necessary condition: they can prove "cannot fit" (`log K < R_IB`); on synthetic hard-wall tasks the lower bound is measured to be tight and locatable |
| ② Output-space discriminability | **closed** — all three positions closed, each with its own principle, not a failure of trial and error (8 readings tried) | Discriminability is not an independent degree of freedom: it either degenerates into a reskin of the capacity bound, or is by construction a function of capacity |
| ③ Training difficulty | **holds, but comparable within a task only** | The normalized cost reading (epochs-to-gate × width factor) ranks configurations within a task; there is no common scale across tasks |

**My three axes are right, but their operability is not symmetric**: ① can produce hard criteria, ③ can rank within the same task, ② after testing cannot exist as an independent criterion. The artifact is a lightweight PyTorch tool that uses **information-theoretic capacity** to answer three questions that otherwise lack answers: **at training time** — is the model capacity sufficient, is it overfitting (capacity diagnosis); **at deployment time** — how many bits can it be quantized to without losing task information (quantization lower bound); **at distillation time** — how small should the student be (distillation capacity lower bound); plus one side product, a **training-in-the-loop configuration optimizer** (turning the "capacity-cost" objective into a prunable search problem). The project is therefore positioned as a **measurement tool + an explicit statement of the applicable domain**; the originally higher goal — "predict before distillation whether the cliff (capacity wall) can be located" — has been exhaustively killed by the consecutive negative results of CP1 → CP3 supplementary test ①.

## 2. Axis ①: information capacity — holds

**Mechanism**: insert a `VQBottleneck(d=64, n_codes=K)` after the representation `T` (without changing the loss or the optimizer), quantize to the codebook by nearest neighbour with straight-through gradients, and read capacity off the relation between codebook entropy `H(Z)` and codebook size `K`. Diagnostic quantities: `H(Z)` (an unbiased O(1) estimate, not the fragile MINE); `K_min = ceil(exp(Fano(1-acc, C)))`, the minimum capacity corresponding to the task's information content; `ρ = K*/K_min`, the capacity surplus ratio (**diagnostic only**).

**Why it holds**: the information-theoretic inequalities (Fano, DPI) are a **necessary condition**; the correct direction in which to use a necessary condition is **refutation** — capacity below what the task requires ⇒ provably cannot fit. This direction was verified to hold repeatedly:

- log K tightness (the P1/C1/T1 three lines): `H(Z)` grows approximately 1:1 with `ln K` — MLP slope 0.95, CNN 0.88, small Transformer 0.88; the Transformer residual-stream correction keeps a narrow bottleneck inside the FFN from locking up (+0.519).
- Task-side demand is computable: `R_IB` is back-derived from the witness's error rate via Fano, `K_min = ceil(e^{R_IB})`; `log K < R_IB` ⇒ DPI + Fano **mathematically forbid** completing the task — a **certified exclusion**, independent of any assumption about "whether the training algorithm can find a solution".
- The Fano lower bound `K_min` is locatable on hard-wall tasks, and the empirical ratio of the distilled measured `K*` to `K_min`, 1.7–3.0, agrees across four cells (CP3); on grid64 `K*=96` is identical across all three seeds (median ratio 1.71, Feature B v3).
- Topology correction: pure serial → `min_capacity_serial`; with a bypass → `sum_capacity_parallel` (each branch "counts on its own"). This is a hard precondition, and using it wrongly badly underestimates: for the same set of serial bottlenecks (min = 1.79 nats), adding one parallel branch moves accuracy 0.103 → 0.876 (P4). P2: taking min holds when there is no bypass; P3: matched capacity converges faster.
- Quantization: Feature A PASS (synthetic linear task, cliff prediction from the quantization lower bound `q_min`); the QAT three-arm control — QAT-vq PASS 3/3, the artifact levels `q=5/6` recover 0.45–0.56 → 0.98+ (removed amount 0.477), QAT-uniform removes only 0.006 ⇒ **all the gain is in quantization-parameter learning, not in classifier adaptation**; at the capacity wall `q=3` all three arms collapse (the DPI+Fano mathematical wall is not rescued by QAT).
- Generalization: C1/C2/C4 (CIFAR-10 + CNN: tightness / width ceiling / Fisher dimension) PASS; T1/T2 (small Transformer: log K tightness / residual stream) slope 0.88.

**Boundary**: the same inequality cannot be used in reverse — "it fits" does not imply "it will work well". It is a necessary condition only, and `R_IB` is **ex-post** (it needs a witness), so it calibrates afterwards and cannot guide "how big to build" beforehand. The entire content of axis ① holding lies on the "lower bound and screening" side. The direction in which it errs is safe: judging "feasible" is at most "the necessary condition is satisfied"; judging "infeasible" is a definite conclusion.

**Applicable domain**: all of axis ①'s validation is on **small-scale/synthetic tasks** (MNIST/CIFAR level or below); there is no validation on real large models. The topology must be determined first. Cliff (capacity wall) location needs only one condition — the task's information content is enough to form a wall (`log K < H(Y)` constitutes a forbidden zone). The original second condition, "the student's representation cluster structure is clear (does not hold for CNN convolutional features)", is **void**: the C3 v3 negative result was re-judged by the CP3 review as a **budget artifact** (the teacher was doubly undertrained: `acc=0.961` right at the gate but the codebook had collapsed to `H(Z_t)=4.18 ≈ ln 64`, only ~65 of the 256 codes used, and the student was undertrained across the whole grid, `g(256)=0.145` a false plateau); under the 40ep calibration budget the same task's cliff is clearly locatable, and all four CP3 cells (MLP/CNN × euclid/cosine) were locatable, `K*/K_min ≈ 1.7–3.0`. The killing of C3-CIFAR still stands, but its reason was refined (see §10.2): it is not "a 10-class task has no wall" — with a strong witness (acc 0.77–0.80) the Fano exclusion is measurable (R1a/R1b 3/3, `K_min=4`) — but **"a wall without a cliff"**: the capacity-accuracy mapping is diffuse, no plateau can be established, `K*` cannot be located — exclusion measurable, location not measurable.

## 3. Axis ②: discriminability — closed (closed for a reason)

Axis ② went through three relocations — predictor → sieve → "third quantity" — each with an explicit hypothesis, pre-registered criteria and a clean negative result; 8 readings were tried in total. **The three closures are not "the right readout has not been found yet"; each layer forced out the same principle.**

### 3.1 The predictor position: 6 candidate readings all falsified

An attempt to find a static readout in representation/parameter space that predicts training difficulty or cliff localizability:

1. representation cluster geometry, silhouette (CP1) — the prediction (predicting before distillation whether the cliff is locatable) was **REFUTED** (the holdout arm failed to validate);
2. codebook-margin dose-response `m = H(Z_t) − ln C` (CP2) — **misjudged**: both fast and full runs fail consistently; `K_t=96` misjudged; with `K_t=128` under full `m` rises into the gray zone and is automatically judged UNDECIDABLE, while under fast it is misjudged; the positive control `K_t=256` hits;
3. the `I(Z;Y)` endpoint (CP3 supplementary test ①) — no resolving power: N=64000 independent evaluation + Miller–Madow gives two CNN cells **exactly equal at 4.033±0.000** while ρ differs by 50%;
4. the rate-distortion knee `K_99` (CP3 supplementary test ①) — coincides at every rung;
5. `L_info` / `L_sys` (Fisher-Rao arc length, CP3 supplementary test ①) — **equivalent-solution drift contaminates the parameter space**: within the same cell the arc length differs by 3.86× across seeds (s2), and the range of the gradient-window displacement, 117, is ≫ the between-cell difference of 64;
6. the detection-theory decision margin `d′` (CP-D, 54 units per task on both tasks) — the 6th falsified candidate. J0 training-reuse self-check PASS (54/54 bit-for-bit identical, the retraining and the main run are homologous); J1 sanity PASS (`frac(m≤0) = 1−acc` bit-for-bit identical); J3 direction + separability **FAIL** (on grid64 the direction fails too, rho −0.29/−0.00/−0.23; on shape64 the direction passes, −0.73/−0.68/−0.82, 3/3 seeds ≤ −0.5, but separability is 0/3 on both tasks); J4 cross-task fails on both tasks (honest negative result); J5 independent value **inconsistent** (grid64 D 0.23 > L_func 0.16 > acc 0.06; shape64 D 0.73 < L_func 0.75 < **acc 0.77**). Reinforcing evidence: the sub-check that fixes metric+width and varies only lr **flips sign** (grid64 `eucl,w64` = −1.00×3 while `cosi,w64` = +0.87/+0.87/+1.00; shape64 `eucl,w16` = −1.00/0.00/+0.87); the between-configuration signal (6.83/7.63) exceeds the within-configuration noise (1.68/2.03), so the failure is **not** noise drowning it out. Stage-0 measurement fixed two degeneracies (both "metric unusable" cases, not "result unsatisfactory"): the probability-difference definition had `MAD = 0` on 1/4 of the units ⇒ the metric is undefined (D blows up to 5e6) ⇒ switched to log-odds; `min_c(class median)` is the extremum of 64 class medians and jumps wildly across seeds (same configuration −1.000 / +0.985) ⇒ switched to the 5% quantile. Definition block: `m(x) = logit(y_true) − max_{j≠y_true} logit(j)`; `S_low = Q_5%(m)`, `noise = MAD(m)`, `D = S_low / noise`; fixed window e0 = 10, target = epochs-to-gate. CP-D is the project's first **clean null** (definition fixed, budget sufficient, data complete, J0/J1 double self-check passed, criteria preceded the data). Methodological caveat: epochs-to-gate contains an acc condition (G0 = acc≥0.95 and H≥threshold), so the high correlation of `acc@10` with it has a **definitional component** — but even setting this aside D shows no ability beyond L_func (0.73 vs 0.75).

**Lesson**: equivalent-solution drift ⇒ parameter-space readouts are in principle not comparable; a **USABLE** readout must live in the functional/predictive space. `L_func` (cumulative prediction flips) is the only reading measured to be USABLE — it is measured in prediction space, where equivalent-solution drift does not contribute — but it is a **process quantity**, not a static discriminability.

### 3.2 The sieve position (CP-E): degenerate at both ends (the 7th reading)

Moving discriminability to the "sieve" position — the accuracy ceiling on the representation (an upper bound no classifier can reach), probed on existing checkpoints with **zero training** (20 units): `ceiling_code` = Bayes-optimal on codes (majority-label rule, `p(y|c)` estimated from train counts); `ceiling_1nn = 1 − err_1nn/2` (via the 1-NN ≤ 2×Bayes bound). E0 sanity (label permutation must collapse to ~1/64) **20/20 PASS** (≤0.018) ⇒ estimator implementation correct; E1a validity **FAIL** (grid64 9/11, shape64 7/9, violation magnitude ≤0.018) — attribution from a control: the results for α∈{1,0.1,0.01,0.001,0} are bit-for-bit identical ⇒ not a smooth bias, but a **finite-sample bias of sparse codes** (when only codes with "training samples ≥3" are counted the violations disappear and the ceiling returns to 1.0000; 3%/7% of test samples fall on codes with <3 training samples); per pre-registration discipline this criterion is recorded as failing as measured, the corrected version serves only as diagnosis; E1b feature upper bound ≥ code upper bound 20/20 PASS; E2 tightness gap ≈ 0 (median 0.000/−0.002) ⇒ no slack; E3 screening power **UNDECIDABLE** (only 1 unit failed the gate, hit 1/1, sample <3, must not be recorded as a negative result); E4 arbitration vs `H(Z_t)` inconsistent across the two tasks (grid64 no value / shape64 apparently better). Two decisive findings (the conclusions do not depend on E1a): `ceiling_1nn ≈ 1.000` holds on all 20 units (1-NN error = 0) ⇒ the 64 classes are fully separable in feature space, the representation is not a constraint at all, and the emptiness of E2/E4 is a **task property**, not an estimator defect; the codebook-compression control (k-means merges codewords into m buckets, prediction preceded the data) gives ceiling m=1:0.010 → 16:0.207 → 32:0.450 → 64:0.718 → 128:0.926 → 256:0.968 against reference `m/64` = 0.016/0.25/0.50/1.0/1.0/1.0.

**Principle**: in the sufficient region the ceiling is constantly ≈1 with no resolving power; in the starved region it is ≡ the capacity bound `m/64`. At this position "discriminability" contributes no information beyond the capacity bound — **it is a reskin of the capacity bound**. This is measurable but degenerate/redundant, different in kind from the first six failures ("unmeasurable / no predictive power").

### 3.3 The "third quantity" position (CP-F / CP-G): by construction a function of capacity

CP-F introduces a labelled nuisance + a perturbation ladder (two routes used together, zero training, 103 units) to separate "signal encoding" from "redundant encoding": annotation nuisance — the generator templates are known, so the brightness gain `g_hat=<x,T(y)>/<T,T>` and the noise residual `r_hat` are recovered exactly per sample, and a ridge-regression probe `t → g_hat/r_hat` gives `r2_br/r2_nz`, `q_nuis = i_sig/(i_sig+i_nuis)`; perturbation ladder — rebuild clean images from `g_hat·T(y)` and sweep noise σ∈{0.15…1.5} and gain η∈{0…0.5} → `stab_drop`, `stab_br_drop`. Three structural findings (independent of any criterion): (1) the representation really does encode nuisance — `r2_br` = 0.47–0.96 (grid64) / 0.59–0.89 (shape64), the first direct measurement of "redundancy" (bits spent on variation the task does not need), exactly what the capacity bound cannot see; (2) the noise-residual probe is numerically ill-conditioned — `r_hat` has variance only 6.6e-3 and 60/61 and 42/42 units fall in |R²|≤0.05 (one case −2.15) ⇒ judged **unusable** (measurement failure, not a negative result), so `i_nuis` is in practice dominated by `r2_br` and `q_br` is reported in parallel (unit-by-unit difference from `q_nuis` <0.005); (3) brightness fragility is capacity-related — `stab_br_drop` is ~0.000 in ample layers and 0.15–0.29 in the starved kt=128 layer, cross-layer rho = −0.912/−0.905 ⇒ BatchNorm-style gain invariance breaks precisely when the codebook is starved, but as a **capacity proxy** rather than a quality reading.

CP-F verdict: F0b sanity (random initialization must land at chance level) **PASS ×2** (acc 0.0203 / ladder end 0.0203 vs 1/64=0.0156); **F1 power** (between configurations > between seeds) **PASS ×2 ×4** — all four readings pass on both tasks (e.g. `q_nuis` 0.097 vs 0.035 / 0.179 vs 0.025), the first time in this project that a suspected quality reading cleared the power gate twice in a row; F2 not a capacity proxy **MIXED** (`q_nuis/q_br` grid64 +0.079 PASS / shape64 **+0.929 judged a capacity proxy**; `stab_drop` +0.261/+0.262 pass on both tasks; `stab_br_drop` −0.912/−0.905 judged a capacity proxy on both); F3 not an acc proxy **MIXED** (`q_nuis/q_br` −0.110/+0.043 both pass; `stab_drop` +0.258 PASS / **+0.890 FAIL**; `stab_br_drop` −0.776/−0.383 both pass); F4 cross-task consistency **FAIL** (`q_nuis` flips on F2, `stab_drop` flips on F3) ⇒ **no generalization claim is permitted**. Net: CP-F FAILS, recorded as a **MIXED result** rather than a clean negative — not emptiness, not redundancy, but "independence flipping with the task"; `q_nuis` passes F1+F2+F3 on grid64, `stab_drop` passes F2 on both tasks; settling it required a third task, not a threshold tweak. Three implementation fixes (all of the "mathematically unusable" kind, each reporting the original verdict as well): (a) F0a's two-sided `|R²_perm|≤0.01` was a criterion bug — a negative R² means no leakage, so the leakage test must be one-sided, and the four out-of-range values −0.89/−0.90/−0.33/−0.15 collapse to a one-sided maximum of +0.021/+0.031; (b) even after the fix the 0.01 threshold is below the statistic's own null-hypothesis noise floor (1/√1920=0.023; the maximum of 122 tests naturally reaches ~2.7σ) ⇒ after calibration (3σ=0.069) both tasks pass = no detectable leakage, the original threshold was a calibration error; (c) `r2_nz` is kept archived and does not enter the verdict.

CP-G (the third task, mnist_gain_mlp: real MNIST-10 data + a synthetically injected per-image brightness gain + a plain MLP — no convolution, no BatchNorm, 10 classes, a different class count; generator template no longer needed, so the method transfers; readings and criteria left word-for-word unchanged): the precondition gate was cleared first — reference configuration median acc **0.9525** ≥ 0.95 (at 8ep 0.9423 failed ⇒ **budget raised to 20ep, the threshold was not lowered**); the first version's VQ design was stopped by the gate (d=128/K=512 20ep only 0.746; d=256/K=2048 fell back to 0.705; utilization 45%→34% — **the constraint is the training budget, not d/K**) and **no criterion was evaluated**. Verdicts: F0a leakage test PASS (one-sided +0.0143 < 3σ=0.0474); F0b **original verdict FAIL** (0.1350 vs 0.1±0.02) → **threshold calibration error** (too tight; its purpose is already met: 0.135 vs 0.88–0.96 after training), recorded honestly, not silently relaxed; **F1 power PASS ×3** (`q_br` 0.1122 vs 0.0100; `stab_drop` 0.0382 vs 0.0052); **F2 FAIL** (`q_br` rho=**−0.960**, `stab_drop` rho=**−0.927**, both judged capacity proxies); F3 PASS (−0.781/−0.598); **F4 FAIL** — `q_br` passes F2 on only one of the three tasks (grid64); on shape64 and on this task it fails. Net: **axis ② is closed together with the "redundancy" position**; CP-F's F4 failure is not an accident of same-family tasks but systematic. Two structural findings: (1) **nuisance encoding grows with capacity — exactly why the ratio cannot escape capacity**: `r2_br` rises monotonically with d (0.41→0.90) while the signal side saturates (`i_sig` 1.68→2.05 ≈ ln10=2.30), so a wider layer encodes both more gain and more classes and `q_br = i_sig/(i_sig+i_nuis)` is **by construction** a monotone function of capacity — axis ②'s third principled closure (CP-E died of degeneracy at both ends, this one of one of its terms growing with capacity); (2) a scalar input gain is algebraically invisible to a ReLU MLP (`fc1(g·x)=g·fc1(x)`, `ReLU(g·a)=g·ReLU(a)`, `fc2(g·h)=g·fc2(h)` ⇒ argmax unchanged; measured `stab_br_drop` ∈ [−0.003,+0.002], judged degenerate) ⇒ the gain-invariance probe must not be used to evaluate any ReLU MLP — the reading is always 0; this refines CP-F finding ③ (there BN and a VQ codebook were present, so the starved layer's gain sensitivity comes from **codebook assignment changing under gain**, not from the network's linear algebra).

**Principle**: the larger the bottleneck, the more nuisance it fits — the "redundant-encoding ratio" is by construction a function of capacity. This is not an experimental failure; it is **a definitional degeneracy of that quantity**.

### 3.4 The closing verdict and its qualifier

> Discriminability as a **third axis independent of capacity** does not exist: at every position where information has resolving power, it is either permanently saturated (sufficient region), or a direct function of the capacity bound (starved region), or grows monotonically with capacity (nuisance encoding). The entire effective content of axis ② has been absorbed by axis ① — discriminability is **measurable, but cannot serve as an independent criterion**.

**2026-09-30 out-of-domain supplement (CP-H axis ②, CIFAR-10 non-saturating region, see `docs/cifar10-anchor.md` §3)**: the closing verdict above was established inside the **saturating region** (grid64's 1-NN error = 0). On a real non-saturating task the S1 premise holds (teacher median err_1nn=0.228, silhouette=0.187 — the closure principle is tested outside its original applicability domain for the first time) and S2 passes ×4 (i_plug 0.92 / silhouette 0.64 / separation 0.68 / d′ 0.60; the readings carry quality information in the knee window, rho(acc) 0.60–0.92, contrasted with the synthetic saturating region's "always constant, no resolving power"). But H_indep is not established (no capacity-independent readout, rho(logK) 0.64–0.82, S3 all gray zone) and H_proxy is not confirmed either (no pure proxy). **The closing verdict needs a qualifier: in the non-saturating region, discriminability and capacity are highly entangled, no independent axis has been found, but the strong form of "pure proxy" does not hold either — the weak form of the closure principle survives.** Arbitration observation (post-hoc, not pre-registered): no reading is better than H(Z)/acc.

## 4. Axis ③: training difficulty — holds, but only within a range

- **The reading**: normalized cost = epochs-to-gate × (conv(w) + K·d), with `conv(w)=1728w²+2560w`, plus early-window `L_func`. The gate is the G0 double gate (`L0` hard constraint: `acc ≥ 0.95` and `H(Z_t) ≥ 0.8·ln K_t`); `L1` primary objective = the cost of reaching the gate; `L2` cheap signal = early-window `L_func@10`.
- **Holds within a task**: the reading + early-window L_func can rank training configurations by cost; the v1.2 normalized signal gives regret = 0 on both the grid64 and shape64 tasks; Successive Halving with `η=3` on the normalized signal reaches regret 0 under the same budget.
- **Not comparable across tasks**: epochs-to-gate depends on the task's gate, and different tasks' gates are not on the same scale; after switching tasks `L_func` is no longer monotone with cost (T4: per-seed `Spearman(L_func@10, normalized cost)` = 0.19 / 0.24 / 0.18, `|r| < 0.3` ⇒ **T4 NO SUPPORT**, recorded with its rank-correlation form as a post-hoc operationalization); the profile shape at the `K_t` knee differs between the two tasks (512 tier 4.2 vs 6.3); shape64's absolute cost is systematically higher than grid64's, but that does not mean "harder", only that the reading differs.
- **The best readout is the cheapest**: CP-D's J5 arbitration shows that for ranking training difficulty `acc@10` (0.77) ≥ `L_func` (0.75) > D (0.73) — a new metric must pass the arbitration bar of "is it better than the cheapest existing readout". Positive by-product (worth recording separately): on shape64, "**training difficulty is readable in function space**" gains strong evidence — `D@10` vs epochs-to-gate gives rho ≤ −0.68 for 3/3 seeds, with the same sign in the early window e0 = 2; the catch is that the best reading is the **cheapest one, `acc@10`** (0.77), not any "new" indicator. This incidentally explains why optimizer v1.0 works with the acc signal and why v1.2 had to add width normalization — **acc can rank difficulty, but it cannot rank cost**.
- **Two corrections the author accepted (data-driven)**: (i) "phase space" must be pinned down as function/prediction space, not parameter space — all readings of parameter space are contaminated by equivalent-solution drift, while readings of prediction space hold; (ii) "becoming more distinguishable" is not a continuing process — **all 12 cells have `flip = 0` in the final epoch** (predictions are completely frozen in the final epoch; flips happen only in the middle of training), which is why **early-window readings (first 2–5 epochs) are instead cleaner than the full set** (they sample while the process is still happening) and why "training difficulty" is a **non-stationary** quantity: what is sampled at different time points is not the same thing.
- **L_func as a reading**: early-window `L_func` is the first valid reading of ρ (capacity fitness) and costs only **1/8–1/20** of a full training run (zero overlap already in the first 2 epochs, 4× margin in the first 5 epochs; 8 seeds, zero overlap, robust across 4 probe sets); it is decoupled from ρ and repositioned as "fitting cost" (the training reorganization cost of a structure-task pair), with ρ downgraded to a diagnostic quantity (multi-level dose). But `L_func@10` width monotonicity **FAILS on both tasks** (grid64 13.0/12.3/17.8; shape64 13.7/19.9/19.4) ⇒ on both tasks, per pre-registration, pruning falls back to the pure `acc@rung` definition, and on the second task L_func serves only as a recorded quantity and does not enter the pruning signal.
- **Width cost makes definitions matter**: w32/w64 cost 3.83×/14.98× more per epoch, and wall clock is not comparable across runs (the same configuration and the same code differed 4× merely because of the measurement time window — cfg1 had the fewest epochs-to-gate yet ranked #11 by wall clock ⇒ the first version's `P5` REFUTED). The cost definition was revised three times and each time flipped a verdict: v1.0 `acc@rung` 3.93; v1.1 raw epochs-to-gate 9.22 (**on its own it looks worse** — the width bias only surfaced after the space was expanded); v1.2 epochs-to-gate × width normalization **0**.

**Optimizer evidence (grid64 = task one; shape64 = task two, switching the encoding modality: 3 strips × 4 shapes → 64 classes, the only difference from grid64 being the encoding modality, so discrimination must happen on the convolutional channels).** Space: `metric × width × lr` (18) + teacher codebook `K_t ∈ {128,256,512,1024,2048}` → **90 configurations in total**; shape64: 72 configurations × 3 seeds = **216 units** (`--layers 256,512,1024,2048`, 80ep budget). Key lever: **"exhaust once → offline exact replay of any scheme"** — early stopping is deterministic, so the curve of the deepest-rung unit is bit-for-bit homologous with the shallow rungs, and one collection run allows any pruning key, seed count or baseline to be replayed with zero extra training; the equivalence was confirmed by measurement in the **foundation self-check** (grid64 25/25 bit-for-bit identical; shape64 3/3 consistent, limited scope: only the "already passed the gate" branch), not assumed.

- grid64: **P2 SUPPORT** (pruning has value: SH regret 3.93 vs 15.82 for equal-budget random search); **P3 SUPPORT** (the `L0` hard constraint makes a real contribution to "selection", 2 vs 4 epochs); P4 collateral damage (gold pruned at rung2/rung3); P6 a draw (multi-fidelity adds no value).
- Capacity greed: `Q1` **REFUTED** (`K_t=128` layer feasible 9/18; the real structure is a **codebook × metric interaction**: all feasible are cosine, all infeasible are euclid); `G1`/`R1` also **REFUTED** (the `K_t=2048` codebook gate of 6.10 **excluded no configuration at all**). But greed self-limits at `K_t=1024`: intra-layer optimal normalized cost `128:7.0 → 256:6.1 → 512:4.2 → 1024:3.4 → 2048:5.0` (**bottoms out and rebounds at 1024**); mechanism: the gate "asks for more epochs" (the intra-layer optimal epoch count rebounds 3→4) **+** the VQ term grows linearly (at 2048 it reaches +27% of `conv(w16)`).
- shape64: **T1 signal generalization (core) SUPPORT** — v1.2 regret = 0, final pick = gold = `cfg56(euclid,w16,lr3e-3,kt1024)`; control: v1.0 acc signal regret 2.95, v1.1 raw epoch count regret 41.25 (it takes w64, width cost not normalized) ⇒ **width normalization is still the decisive item**. **T2 greedy self-limiting reproduces: SUPPORT** — intra-layer optimal `256:6.1 → 512:6.3 → 1024:3.4 → 2048:5.0`, turning point at 1024; three layers (256/1024/2048) are bit-for-bit identical to grid64 (6.1 / 3.4 / 5.0) and only the 512 layer differs (4.2 vs 6.3); same mechanism, the optimal configuration's epoch count rebounds 3→4 and the VQ rent is +11.9%. **T3 G0 boundary reproduces: PARTIAL** — the `K_t=256` anchor layer is feasible 18/18 (reproduced); the **codebook × metric interaction is UNDECIDABLE** (on grid64 that interaction appears at the `K_t=128` layer, which was not collected this round). **T4: NO SUPPORT** (see above). Accompanying same-direction reproductions: **G1** — the gate still does not bite, the 2048 layer has **54/54 units pass** `H(Z_t) ≥ 6.10` (measured 6.11–6.52); **R1** — still excludes nothing (18/18); **G3** — the signal did not take the largest `K_t`: greed is stopped by **cost**, not by the gate.
- Net conclusion: both the optimizer v1.2 signal design and v2's two claims — "the gate only forms a soft cost upper bound" and "greed has an intrinsic self-limit" — generalize to the second task after the encoding modality is switched; the only thing that does not generalize is L_func (T4), and it never entered the pruning signal in the first place. The gate does not exclude configurations; under the cost definition greed has an intrinsic optimum and the space does not need to be artificially limited.

**Interface with architecture research (A-002, 2026-10)**: the decomposition "big mapping → routing function × a family of local functions" (QKV's content addressing, MoE's parameter routing) **does not change the capacity lower bound; what it changes is the cost of reaching the upper bound** — interference falls, reachability rises. Hence **architectural differences are the content of axis ③**: any assertion that "architecture A is better than architecture B" holds only under the same task and the same budget reading. This also answers "internal structure is only a channel, so why is architecture evolution still worth studying" — architecture does not decide "whether it can" (that is axis ①), it decides "whether it can be found, and at what cost" (axis ③).

## 5. Overall verdict: measurement holds, prediction is killed

| | Measurement (after the fact) | Prediction (before the fact) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Status | **holds** | **killed** |
| Basis | the capacity lower bound, the training cost and the cliff position are all measurable, and replicate bit-for-bit across tasks | all 6+ predictive readouts are falsified; all three relocations of axis ② fall back to capacity |

The principle of the divide: information theory gives a **necessary condition** (a lower bound); a necessary condition is good at refuting ("cannot fit") and bad at affirming ("if it fits it will work well"). This project turned that divide from a slogan into an experiment-by-experiment empirical boundary. The wall and `K*` readings stay **protocol-relative** (they depend on the training recipe, the budget, the student family and the seed), so their measurement conditions must be quoted with them; the certified exclusion does not.

## 6. The answer to the "task↔network correspondence judgment"

Testing "do task T and network N match" with the three axes:

- **Can be judged**: capacity insufficient (`H(Y)` or `R_IB` exceeds the bottleneck's upper bound) ⇒ definitively does not match. This is a hard judgment, already demonstrated on several tasks, and the direction in which it errs is safe (judging "feasible" is at most "the necessary condition is satisfied").
- **Cannot be judged**: of two that both have enough capacity, which matches better; whether training goes smoothly once matched; whether the cliff is locatable — these need the predictive power of axis ②/③, and axis ② is closed while axis ③ is not comparable across tasks.
- That is: the **negative half** of the correspondence judgment holds, the **affirmative half and the ranking half** do not.

Two concrete questions judged feasible (they are necessary-condition-type questions rather than predictions): **(a) guiding network distillation** — judge whether a given student can complete the teacher's task; the key reason is that distillation is the scenario where the limitation "post-hoc quantity" is not an obstacle (the teacher exists by nature, and `R_IB` can be measured at no extra cost); measured empirical margin `K*/K_min ≈ 1.7–3.0` (grid64 single task 1.71, effective dose 9/9, CP3 four cells 1.7–3.0 consistent). **(b) using "task complexity" to judge whether a network can complete this task** — `H(Y) = ln C` (class-count entropy, coarse-grained complexity: a **pure task property, known at design time**, but only an upper bound, ignoring inter-class correlation); `R_IB` (the information the task **actually requires**: operational definition, but a **lower-bound estimate** needing a witness); the gap `R_IB − log K` (feasibility gap, the direct basis for judgment). The directionality to hold: because `R_IB` is a lower bound, "below it ⇒ infeasible" is reliable while "above it ⇒ feasible" does not hold (it is only the necessary condition being satisfied).

```
R_IB = H(Y) − h(err_teacher) − err_teacher · ln(C−1)   # Fano, using the teacher's error rate
K_min = ceil(e^{R_IB})                                  # the student's capacity lower bound
criterion: log K_student < R_IB  ⇒ definitely cannot do it (no need to train)
```

**What the project can and cannot answer**: it can answer whether capacity is enough, the quantization lower bound, the distillation lower bound, the capacity upper bound (the topology must be determined first), and the cost ranking of configurations within the same task; it cannot answer using any information-theoretic/geometric/detection quantity to predict performance or cliff localizability. The dividing line is clear: **anything asking "at least how much is needed" can be answered; anything asking "is this good enough / would it be better" cannot.**

**Statement boundaries to hold externally**: only lower bounds, only screening, promise no prediction; all evidence is on synthetic / small-scale tasks (synthetic grid64/shape64 + MNIST/CIFAR demo) and real large-scale tasks are unvalidated; `R_IB` is a **post-hoc quantity** (it needs a witness) ⇒ it can only calibrate afterwards, it cannot guide "how big to build" beforehand; the preconditions are **tiered by the judgment** — **exclusion verdicts are zero-training** (only the witness's test accuracy + the candidate structure's capacity upper bound are needed), the wall-geometry pre-check is zero-training (one forward pass of the witness), and only "measuring the capacity of a given network" and "locating `K*`" require inserting a bottleneck and retraining (a diagnostic scaffold); three preconditions are indispensable — **the task has a wall**, **the topology is judged correctly**, **the witness is strong enough** (otherwise `R_IB` is underestimated ⇒ a false "feasible").

The loop was closed on 2026-09-30 (CP-H / PA1; verdict report in `docs/cifar10-anchor.md` §2): the distillation criterion (`K_min` + the gap curve) holds on synthetic hard-wall tasks and (1) it was validated on a real task — **the exclusion capability transfers** (R1a/R1b 3/3, R4 −0.98×3: every student below `K_min=4` is mathematically crushed, operable screening), **cliff localization does not transfer** (plateau 1/3, the only `K*=128`, margin 32× — more than ten times the synthetic 1.7–3.0), refining the usage boundary to "the half that judges a mismatch holds on a real task; the half that locates the minimum feasible student still belongs exclusively to hard-wall tasks"; (2) the task-complexity quantity is operable — `K_min` is piecewise-quantized by teacher acc (acc < 0.715 ⇒ `K_min≤3` degenerate; 0.715–0.807 ⇒ `K_min=4`; >0.807 ⇒ ≥5) and the 0.78 gate calibration rests precisely on "screening out the degenerate regime"; the degeneracy of 10-class tasks is not "no wall" but **"a wall but no cliff"** — exclusion measurable, localization not; (3) the deliverable use holds: **judging whether a task and a network match, and giving a provable exclusion reason when they do not** (the Fano exclusion holds 3/3 on a real task). Two corrections to the original judgments (accepted): "10-class tasks have no wall ⇒ invalid" — wrong: the wall exists, only cliff localization fails; "`K_t=128` is finally invalid" — wrong (the PA1 ladder proves 120ep reaches the gate and 200ep is all 1.0): that was axis ③'s "thin margin + high cost", not a final verdict of the capacity-pressure regime. Both are **budget/framing artifacts**, confirming once again that **a negative result itself can also be wrong**.

## 7. Why "how to build it well" cannot be learned

**"does it fit" is a mathematical inequality about all networks (provable, excludable); "building it well" is an empirical fact about one particular training path (measurable only afterwards).** For "how to build it well" to become a learnable target, four necessary conditions must hold simultaneously; the project refutes them one by one:

| Necessary condition | Status | Measured evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) The objective already exists at decision time | **not satisfied** — cost/quality are **properties of a process** | one example: `H(Z)=2.80 > R_IB=2.30` says it fits, yet acc is only **0.746** |
| (b) The objective corresponds one-to-one with an observable | **not satisfied** — **equivalent solutions** give the same function infinitely many parameter realizations | the arc length differs by 3.86× in the same cell, range 117 ≫ between-cell difference 64, while the predictions are identical |
| (c) The regularity is stable across tasks | **not satisfied** — measured cross-task flips | epochs-to-gate cannot cross tasks; `L_func` is no longer monotone after switching tasks; `q_br` passes the capacity-independence gate in only one of three tasks |
| (d) The objective function is given | **not satisfied** — "good" is **a framing chosen by a person** | the cost framing was **revised three times, and each time it flipped the verdict** |

| Framework-level condition (source §7) | Status | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A static readout comparable across configurations exists | ✗ the parameter space is contaminated by equivalent-solution drift | CP3 supplementary test ① |
| A discriminability signal independent of capacity exists | ✗ all three positions closed | §3 |
| A difficulty scale comparable across tasks exists | ✗ comparable within a task only | §4 |
| The predictive signal beats the cheapest existing readout | ✗ all lose to acc@rung | CP-D J5 |

All four are unsatisfied ⇒ it is not "not enough data" or "the wrong model", but that **the signal basis for that learning target does not exist**. More sharply: **the only reading proven effective is a "process quantity"** (`L_func`), and a process quantity must be **run through once first** before it can be measured — it is not computed, it is recorded. The reinforcement-learning route (using this project as an environment to train a policy that tunes hyperparameters) is the same: its value would lie only in **policy transfer across tasks**, and (c) is empirically denied; RL needs **a reward evaluable at decision time**, and (a) does not hold; if the reward takes a predictive quantity it has already been falsified, and if it takes a cost quantity it degenerates into the already-solved ranking problem (bandit/SH territory — under the same budget SH's regret can reach 0). Hence the two directions no longer advocated: using information theory to **predict network performance / predict cliff localizability** (that line is exhausted — the 6 readings of ②), and using reinforcement learning to **train a tuning policy that learns how to build networks** (for the reason above, which is more fundamental than "the space can be exhausted").

## 8. Negative results and the meta-lesson

### 8.1 Negative results that were themselves overturned

- **C3 v3**: the first verdict was an honest negative result (on continuous convolutional representations the VQ compression cost decays smoothly with `log K`, there is no cliff, `K*` location 0/3); the CP3 review re-judged it a budget artifact (numbers in §2).
- **The `K_t=128` layer "definitively invalid"**: the archived plateau (40ep 0.73–0.83; after 80ep recalibration still 0.83–0.90 with diminishing incremental gain) was **overturned by the PA1 ladder (2026-09-30)** — rerun on GPU, all seeds reach the gate at 120ep and all are 1.0000 at 200ep ⇒ the plateau was a trajectory/budget artifact, and this is axis ③'s "thin margin + high cost" (reachable at ~120ep).
- **grid64's P2 once read "pruning has no value"**: later overturned by the definition revision — the raw epoch-count definition looked only at epochs, not at the fact that w32/w64 cost 3.83×/14.98× more per epoch, so random search looked "good" by hitting wide configurations through its diversity.
- **Three predictions Q1 / G1 / R1 REFUTED**: Q1 assumed the `K_t=128` layer was "almost entirely infeasible" (actually 9/18); G1/R1 assumed the gate of 6.10 must exclude the 2048 layer (actually 18/18 all pass). Common error: **mistaking "the value read when training stopped early" for "the capability ceiling"**.
- **Engineering problems of the shape64 round (all fixed and archived)**: the first round's data-pipeline bug — `make_data` passed `seed` into `sigma_pix` as a positional argument, so it actually ran as a "σ sweep" (s0=σ0 no noise, everything passes the gate; s1/s2=σ1.0/2.0 noise drowns the signal), and the three seeds shared the same noise realization ⇒ **all 53 units of the first round were void** (fixed to `make_data(n, seed=seed)`); the layer-limited replay pool was not narrowed (`--layers` collected only 54 configurations but the replay used `pool=None` over 90, `n_keep = 90//3^(r+1)` ⇒ the `η=3` pruning ratio was implicitly relaxed to ~1.8; fixed by `collect_pool()`); "no data" read as "infeasible" (`Q1`/`G3` reported the uncollected `K_t=128`/`2048` layers as "0/18 infeasible / already blocked"; unified as UNDECIDABLE plus a new `blocked` marker for "has data but 0 feasible"); pre-registered ≠ implemented (v3.0's T1–T4 existed only as docstring pre-registration and the reporting code did not output them; T4's rank-correlation form is a post-hoc operationalization, as declared); the foundation self-check was skipped entirely in the first `--layers` run (only the deepest rung trained, no online shallow-rung control ⇒ not passed; supplemented with genuinely trained ep20 units, 3/3 consistent, scope limited to the "already passed the gate" branch, where the same code path on grid64 was 25/25).

**Meta-lesson**: `P2`'s "negative result" came from a **definition error**; C3 v3's from a **budget artifact**; the "positive conclusions" of `Q1`/`G1`/`R1` from **reading missing data as a negative result**. **Every verdict must be able to answer: by what definition, what budget, and what data completeness is this reading defined?** If any one of the three is wrong, the verdict's direction can flip. Conversely, CP-D is the project's first **"clean null"** (definition fixed, budget sufficient, data complete, J0/J1 double self-check passed, criteria preceded the data — the indicator simply does not work). The four situations together say: **before judging something negative, the first three must be ruled out; only the negative results that survive that are trustworthy.**

### 8.2 Methodological lessons (transferable)

1. **The definition precedes the conclusion** — the cost definition was revised three times and each time changed a verdict; wall clock is **not comparable across runs** (the same configuration and the same code differed 4× because of the measurement time window); when changing a definition, **the signal, the primary objective and the sensitivity check** must be aligned together, otherwise cross-definition comparisons necessarily produce wrong conclusions.
2. **When comparing across groups, the seed sets must be exactly identical** — a different seed count is contamination, not "more data is better": probing the boundary with a single seed made T2 read the **opposite** conclusion from 3 seeds (1 seed profile `6.1/6.3/4.5/3.8` ⇒ no turning point; 3 seeds `6.1/6.3/3.4/5.0` ⇒ turning point present), because the intra-layer optimum is the median over the 18 configurations and `median-of-3` jumps between 3 and 4 epochs.
3. **"No data" ≠ "infeasible"** — without a guard, `0/18` gets read as "the whole layer is infeasible", writing uncollected data as a negative result; the verdict layer must explicitly distinguish three states: not collected (UNDECIDABLE) / collected but 0 feasible (hard exclusion) / has feasible configurations.
4. **A space-reduction experiment must recompute the budget over the actually collected set** — when `--layers` limits the layers, `n_keep`, the nominal budget and the baseline sampling must all be narrowed, otherwise the pruning ratio is implicitly relaxed (`η=3` → ~1.8) and the equal-budget comparison is distorted.
5. **Boundary probing with a small sample can only answer qualitative questions** — a single seed can answer "is this layer feasible" but not the turning-point location, which needs fine ranking; establish in advance what level of conclusion a probe can support.
6. **Pre-registration written into a docstring ≠ the criterion is implemented** — the reporting code must be checked to see whether it really outputs that criterion.
7. **The pattern of multi-seed failures is itself a diagnostic signal** — if "the failure level depends only on the seed and is unrelated to every hyperparameter", check the **data pipeline** first rather than the optimization dynamics (the positional-argument bug raised no error and the data statistics looked perfectly normal).
8. **Keyword arguments of data-class functions must be passed explicitly** (`seed=seed`) — positional passing silently misaligns when default parameters sit in the middle of the signature.
9. **Signal quality matters more than budget** — when the pruning signal and the objective definition are misaligned, pruning performs **worse than random search**.
10. **Offline replay is a compute-saving lever, but it must first be verified by measurement** (the foundation self-check); otherwise the whole set of conclusions rests on an unverified equivalence.
11. **The self-check itself must guard against being "trivially true"** — promotion-copy records are bit-for-bit identical to the shallow rung by construction, so comparing them is trivially true and they are not counted.
12. **A new indicator must pass the "is it better than the cheapest existing reading" gate (the arbitration criterion), otherwise it should not exist** — CP-D's J5 was set up for exactly this: d′ is indeed strongly correlated with training difficulty on shape64 (rho ≤ −0.68), but **in the same window `acc@10` (0.77) and `L_func@10` (0.75) are both no worse**; **"correlated" does not equal "useful"; useful = cheaper than the others and more informative than them.**
13. **Indicator engineering must be staged** — check three things in the zero-training/free stage (implementation sanity; degeneracy, e.g. `MAD=0`/None/explosion; cross-seed stability) and only then spend compute on confirmation (CP-D's stage 0 caught both "mathematically unusable" definitions for free).
14. **Changing the language of the reading can bypass saturation, but it does not automatically buy validity** — CP1 → CP-D was "entropy quantities saturate ⇒ switch to a detection quantity": the direction was right, the result was still negative; bypassing one lesion only solves "can it be read", not "is what is read useful".
15. **Before moving a reading from the "predictor" position to the "sieve" position, check the two endpoints** — in the ample regime, is it always constant (then it carries no information); in the starved regime, does it coincide with an existing bound (then it is redundant). CP-E has it both ways, which is also why "switch to a quantity that is easier to measure" usually cannot revive a killed route.
16. **The threshold of a sanity criterion must be reconciled against the statistic's own null-hypothesis noise floor** — CP-F's F0a originally set `|R²_perm|≤0.01`: it was written two-sided by mistake, and that threshold is below the `1/√n_test = 0.023` noise floor while the maximum of ~122 tests naturally reaches ~2.7σ. **Amending a criterion must be able to state "why this is mathematically unusable", not "the result is unsatisfactory"** — all 4 criterion amendments in this project (2 in CP-D stage 0, the F0a threshold in CP-F, the F0b threshold in CP-G) were recorded by this standard, each reporting the original verdict at the same time.
17. **"Power" (can it discriminate) and "independence" (is it merely a proxy for an existing quantity) are two separate gates** and cannot substitute for each other — CP-F's four readings all cleared the power gate on both tasks, yet their independence flipped with the task: **a reading with resolving power can still be a proxy**.
18. **A task must first pass its precondition gate; a design stopped by the gate must not evaluate any criterion** — CP-G's VQ version exposed already at calibration that it "cannot enter the trained regime" (d=128/K=512 20ep only 0.746; d=256/K=2048 fell back to 0.705, utilization 45%→34% ⇒ **the constraint is the training budget, not the architecture**), and running the criteria would only yield a task artifact; the handling is to redesign the task (drop the VQ, switch the capacity axis to width d) rather than lower the threshold or tweak the criteria — the gate itself was raised from 0.9423 to 0.9525 by **adding budget (8ep→20ep)**. Criteria only become meaningful after "the task holds".
19. **A threshold must be aligned with the statistic's units and noise floor, and "the purpose is met" cannot substitute for "the threshold passes"** — CP-G's F0b set 0.1±0.02, random initialization measured 0.1350 ⇒ the criterion fails although its purpose (0.135 vs 0.88–0.96 after training) is clearly met; the way to record this is to report the original verdict together with the calibration note, **not to silently relax it**.

## 9. Applicable domain, forbidden zone and honest boundaries

### 9.1 Can be used

- Capacity diagnosis (`H(Z)` vs `ln K` tightness).
- Deployment quantization bit-width lower bound (`q_min`), with QAT available to remove PTQ calibration artifacts.
- Distillation capacity lower bound (`K*` / `K_min`), provided the task has a hard wall and the student's representation cluster structure is clear (the second condition was voided by CP3/CP-H, see §2; the surviving requirement is the hard wall).
- Capacity upper-bound estimation — **the topology must be determined first** (serial min / with bypass sum).
- Ranking the "cost" of training configurations (normalized-cost definition), and reading "fitting cost" with early-window `L_func`.

### 9.2 Cannot be used / unreliable

- Using `ρ` or any static reading to **predict** whether a cliff is locatable (already killed); and using the decision margin `d′` (or any "discriminability" reading) to predict training difficulty — it is indeed correlated (rho ≤ −0.68 for 3/3 seeds on shape64) but no better than the cheapest `acc@10` and does not replicate across tasks (CP-D, §3.1).
- Applying `min_capacity_serial` to a network with skip/residual/parallel branches (it will severely underestimate).
- Assuming "the gate can exclude large-codebook configurations" — the gate does **not** exclude; it only forms a soft upper bound through cost.
- Expecting `L_func` to remain monotonically correlated with cost after a task change (T4 NO SUPPORT; width monotonicity FAILS on both tasks).
- Comparing wall-clock time across runs; and treating "the value read when training stopped early" as the capability ceiling.
- Using the "representation accuracy ceiling" to screen representations — measurable (E0 sanity 20/20) but degenerate: always 1 when capacity is ample (1-NN error is 0 in all 20 units) and equivalent to the capacity bound `m/64` when capacity is starved (CP-E, §3.2). **To judge "is this representation enough", use the capacity bound (axis ①) directly; do not take the detour through the accuracy ceiling.**
- Treating CP-F's readings as established quality indicators — they pass F1+F2+F3 on grid64, but on shape64 `q_nuis` degenerates into a capacity proxy (rho=+0.929) and `stab_drop` into an acc proxy (rho=+0.890), and F4 FAILS (CP-F, §3.3); **they are not USABLE criteria**, and using the "signal/redundancy ratio" (`q_nuis`/`q_br`) or perturbation fragility as a capacity-independent quality indicator fails the same way — of the three tasks `q_br` passes F2 on only one (grid64), the other two are judged capacity proxies (shape64 +0.929, mnist_gain_mlp −0.960), and the mechanism is that **nuisance encoding grows monotonically with capacity** (`r2_br` 0.41→0.90 as d=8→128), so the ratio depends on capacity by construction (CP-G, §3.3).
- Using the noise residual `r_hat` as a redundancy probe — that target has variance only 6.6e-3 and its R² is numerically ill-conditioned (CP-F structural finding 2).
- Using the "gain invariance" probe to evaluate any MLP with ReLU — a scalar input gain is **algebraically invisible** (`fc1(g·x)=g·fc1(x)`, `ReLU` positively homogeneous, `fc2` linear ⇒ argmax unchanged); the reading is always 0 and carries no information (CP-G structural finding 2).

### 9.3 Honest boundaries (must accompany any external material)

1. All conclusions are on **synthetic tasks** (synthetic grid64 / shape64, CNN backbone) plus the real-task addenda CP-H (CIFAR-10) and CP-G (MNIST); no real large-scale task validation.
2. The optimizer line uses **3 seeds**.
3. **When the signal and the criterion are isomorphic, a "hit" does not constitute independent verification** — parts of the T1 and P11 conclusions are linkage/tautology checks.
4. **The foundation self-check has limited scope** — on shape64 only 3 control points, covering only the "already passed the gate" branch (grid64 was 25/25).
5. `T4`'s rank-correlation form is a **post-hoc operationalization** (pre-registration only said "per-seed separable").
6. `T3`'s codebook × metric interaction is **UNDECIDABLE** (the `K_t=128` layer is missing).
7. `P8/P13` holdout generalization is **UNDECIDABLE** (seed count < 6).
8. Epochs-to-gate **cannot be compared across tasks** (the cost structure of the width axis differs between the two tasks).
9. The shape set and the strip positions are design choices (degrees of freedom); shape area is partially correlated with class (9/3/3/5, so partial shortcuts exist).

## 10. CP-H / PA1 / PA4 / MOE addendum (2026-09-30)

> The full verdict report is in `docs/cifar10-anchor.md`; the pre-registered criteria are in the docstrings of `experiments/cph_demo.py` and `experiments/pa1_demo.py`. Everything was done on a borrowed GPU environment, with relay mode in effect throughout.

### 10.1 PA1 (P-A1-3, verdict A)

Budget ladder (`K_t=128`, grid64 CNN-euclid, 3 seeds, 40→200ep): all three seeds **reach the gate at 120ep and are all 1.0000 at 200ep** ⇒ **A-001's corollary passes its most direct test** — the case of "enough capacity but unable to spread out" does not exist. `K_t=128` is A-002's "thin margin + high cost" (axis ③): the nominal channel, ln 128 ≈ 4.85 nats, is only 0.69 nats wider than the task's wall, H(Y) ≈ 4.16 nats, and reachability is slow (~120ep vs ~40ep for a healthy layer), but given the budget it reaches 1.0. The archived "plateauing" at 40/80ep is a **trajectory-fork artifact** and has been overturned (a CPU rerun at 40ep is bit-for-bit identical to the archive and diverges at 80ep; the GPU/CPU trajectories diverge from epoch 1 = an initialization-family difference). Incidentally: from 160→200ep `H(Z_t)` goes 4.63→4.32 while acc→1.0 — the late compression is not task entropy (clearance 0.48→0.16), exactly what the closure principle should look like.

### 10.2 CP-H axis ① (transfer of distillation exclusion to a real task)

CIFAR-10 + SmallCNN(width96), teacher K=256/120ep/cosine, G0 threshold 0.78 (calibrated on the quantized `K_min`), grid `K∈{2…256}` in 13 levels, 3 seeds: **R1a/R1b 3/3, R4 −0.98/−0.98/−0.99** — the Fano exclusion criterion **holds** on a real task and operates as a screening rule (smallest gap 0.56–0.62); the teacher's `K_min=4` for all three seeds, matching the threshold-quantization prediction bit for bit. **Cliff location does not transfer**: plateau established 1/3 (all stuck at the edge of the 0.02 tolerance), the only `K*=128` ⇒ **margin 32×** (more than ten times the synthetic 1.7–3.0); the knee is diffuse over K=8–32; the long-tail decay is the equal-budget cost of the KD objective (K=256 twin student 0.752 vs teacher 0.799). **G0 2/3** (seed1 0.768, short by 0.012, within one noise band): flagged honestly, the threshold untouched, and **re-rolling to pass the gate refused**; seed 1's G0 is archived **RESTRICTED**. Archive correction: "a 10-class task has no wall ⇒ the tool fails" is refined to **"a wall (exclusion measurable) without a cliff (location not measurable)"**.

### 10.3 CP-H axis ② and new methodology

The out-of-domain test of the closure principle is reported in §3.4 (S1 premise holds: err_1nn=0.228, silhouette=0.187; S2 ×4 passes; S3 all gray zone, rho(logK) 0.64–0.82 ⇒ H_indep not established, H_proxy not confirmed, the weak form survives). New methodology from this round:

- **`K_min` is quantized**: it suffices that the acc threshold falls inside a quantization interval (the chain of reasoning behind CP-H's 0.78 threshold calibration); a threshold should be calibrated on the **regime** it screens out (a degenerate `K_min`), not on a specific value inside a noise band.
- **Run-to-run variance of CUDA training is ±0.03** (same seed, same configuration); the discrete branch of VQ dead-code reset amplifies cuDNN nondeterminism, so calibration numbers carry that noise themselves, while checkpoint replay is unaffected (cached units are only loaded, never retrained).
- **Relay mode (default) + additive grid extension** (the grid fingerprint contains only the base grid, and top-end extensions reuse the cache) — a generalization of the opt_demo v2 precedent.
- **Leftovers**: the 14-point partial-correlation test of CP-H axis ② (rho(reading, acc | logK)) was not done — the only possible route to adjudicate the gray zone further, and it needs more units; the plateau tolerance 0.02 sits at the margin on real tasks (0.025 vs 0.02), so future reruns should pre-register a task-calibrated tolerance; all CP-H conclusions are single-task (CIFAR-10) + 3 seeds.

### 10.4 PA4 (P-A1-4, verdict SUPPORT) and A-003's two predictions

- **Proposition** (the A-003 opened entry, `docs/output-mapping-discreteness.md`): cliff sharpness is a function of **output-mapping discreteness**, not a property of capacity; the Fano exclusion is independent of discreteness.
- **Experiment**: grid64 with a diffusion knob δ∈{0,0.15,0.3,0.5} (50/50 blending with another class's template) × 3 seeds, the grid extended downward by {4,6} for 10 levels in total.
- **Results (Q1–Q3 all pass)**: locatability 0.67→0.33→0.33→0.00 and median sharpness 0.62→0.60→0.53→0.40 are monotonically non-increasing; `err_1nn` 0.000→0.114→0.223→0.390 rises monotonically and cleanly crosses 0.02; Q1 excludes 12/12 (`K_min` adapts downward with δ, 35→6); `DISCRETE_THRESHOLD=0.02` in `wall_geometry_probe` is **formally calibrated**. **Protocol-fix accounting**: the first round's "refutation" at δ=0.5 was a grid-coverage defect (`K_min` below the grid bottom of 8) misread as a violation; after extending the grid and separating "undecidable" from "violation", everything passes — **a defect is not a scientific conclusion**.
- **Theoretical significance**: CP-H's "a wall without a cliff" gains a **controlled reproduction and a mechanistic attribution**, and the conflated premise "the task's information content is enough to form a wall" is split into (a) information content ⇒ a wall exists / (b) discreteness ⇒ the cliff is sharp (**exclusion depends on (a), location on (b)**). Ledger: P-A1-4 has been checked against data (**SUPPORT**).
- **P-A3-1 checked against data (SUPPORT)**: on MNIST (which never took part in calibration) the probe predicted diffuse×3 and no meaningful cliff was measured (meaningful `K*` 1/3 < 2/3) — **the probe's first positive prospective pass**; incidentally confirming that MNIST is a diffuse-wall task. The discrete-wall arm of the positive direction still awaits new data.
- **P-A3-2 checked against data (PARTIAL SUPPORT, recorded as-is)**: two-definition rho=+0.936 (≥0.8 PASS); classification agrees on 53/54 on both sides of 0.02 — the only violation is the undertrained δ=0 teacher that failed G0 (representation separable, readout undertrained) ⇒ **the representation definition is over-optimistic for undertrained witnesses**; deployment discipline = pass the G0 precondition check before probing.

### 10.5 MOE (two-tier capacity reading validation, 2026-09-30, route64 + oracle routing, 6 seeds)

- **Task/design**: route64 (8 clusters × 8 classes, cluster = the block's row, routing information exactly ln8); oracle routing (computable from the input) + 8 experts (`VQ(K_e)`+head); dense control = `GridCNN(8·K_e)`. Flow-tier bound = ln8 + lnK_e, storage tier = 8·lnK_e.
- **The first round's pre-registration (T1a/T1b anchored at the Fano wall ln64) is REFUTED**: ke=8 (flow=4.16=the wall) reaches only 0.57-0.60; the cliff is actually at ke=16/32. **Root cause = wrong reference anchoring**: the dense control itself shows this task family's practical wall is at K=256 (K=64→0.39-0.60, K=128→0.69-0.90, K=256→0.999) — the Fano lower bound underestimates the practical wall by 4× (the same origin as CP-H's 32× margin).
- **Re-anchored follow-up (new seeds 5-7, F1 criteria pre-registered) verdict SUPPORT**: F1a 2/2 judgeable seeds (measured wall 256 → predicted ke=32 → routed 1.000/0.999); the third seed had no detectable dense wall (its dense student 0.715) but routed(32)=0.998 overtook its strongest dense — recorded as "undecidable", not a failure; **F1b 3/3** (routed ≥ dense(8·K_e)−0.05 at every level: equal-total-codebook routing is no worse, and better at high levels = evidence for A-002's interference mechanism).
- **Toolkit conclusion**: `conditional_capacity` is upgraded to **VERIFIED (route64 task family)** — the storage tier passes T2, the flow-tier formula holds, and **the anchor must be the measured dense wall, not the Fano lower bound**; `kv_storage_capacity` remains a **CANDIDATE** (attention tasks are out of scope, recorded as-is).
