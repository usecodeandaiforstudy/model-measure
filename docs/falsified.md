## III. Development log (historical trail, not current conclusions)

> The following is the full text of the original README "development log", accumulated day by day; the verdicts in each paragraph marked "void / revised / old record" have all been overturned by later work, and are kept to show the revision trail. Current conclusions are framework.md / cifar10-anchor.md that prevail.

**v0.2.0 / v2 / QAT / v3 (2026-09-16 to 09-20)**: MVP complete; v1's Fisher module implemented and unit-tested; full MNIST/CIFAR validation run. **All v2 planned items complete (including the full rerun)**: capacity correction for residual/bypass topologies (P4 full passes on the whole set, `sum_capacity_parallel` added); the small-Transformer line (T1/T2 full pass: T1 slope 0.88, T2 lift +0.519), and along the way the real defect "the bottleneck layer only supports 2-D tensors" was fixed; multi-seed reruns complete (feature A 3/3 pass; feature B passes after the v3 redo: switched to the grid64 hard-wall task, K\*=96 identical across three seeds, median ratio 1.71); **QAT passes (full 3 seeds)**: three-arm control — QAT-vq passes J1∧J2 3/3, the artifact tier q=5/6 recovers from 0.45–0.56 to 0.98+ (removed amount 0.477), QAT-uniform 0/3 (removed amount 0.006) → "the gain is entirely in quantization-parameter learning"; the capacity wall q=3 drops in all three arms. **v2 wrap-up**: C3 redone — the pre-flight probe confirms that the information content of CIFAR-10's 10 classes is insufficient to form a wall (teacher acc=0.559 → Fano K_min=2, hard ceiling 10), so the work switched to the synthetic-image grid64 hard-wall task; **v3 full (2026-09-20): the G0 encoding gate and the wall directionality pass (teacher 1.000×3, K_min=64, the gap column flips exactly at 64), but K\* localization is 0/3** — the VQ compression cost decays smoothly with log K, no cliff; the metric probe (euclid) confirms the metric is not the root cause (the teacher is instead under-converged at 0.839 and the student overtakes it) → **C3 closes as an honest negative result** (that negative result was later re-judged by the CP3 re-check as a budget artifact, see section II). **ρ reading wrap-up (2026-09-23, CP3 supplementary test)**: all candidate readings that explain the capacity-adaptation ratio ρ are falsified, and the reading that survived is **L_func (accumulated prediction flips)** — 8 seeds seed-by-seed zero overlap, robust across 4 probe sets, fully separable in the first 5 epochs (1/8 of the training cost). **Multi-level dose decoupling (2026-09-23)**: on K_t∈{256,512}, ρ stays flat while L_func decreases monotonically (3.59→1.19) — L_func is decoupled from ρ and repositioned as the **fitting cost**; ρ is retained as a surplus diagnostic. After adding the K_t=128 tier (80ep), acc still does not pass the gate (plateau at 0.83–0.90) and is finally invalid (**later overturned by PA1, see section II**) — the conclusion at the time: G0 is the boundary of the feasible region.

**Optimizer v1–v1.2 wrap-up (2026-09-23)**: three reward layers (L0 G0 hard constraints / L1 cost-to-gate / L2 early-window L_func) + SH pruning with η=3 (rungs 10/20/40), with a space of metric×width×lr, 18 configurations in total. The key lever is **"exhaustively enumerate once → replay any scheme exactly offline"** (confirmed by the ground-floor self-check 25/25 bit-for-bit identical). Main experiment conclusions: ① **P2 judged at the time that "pruning has no value", later overturned by a framing revision** — the original epochs framing ignores the per-epoch width cost, and under v1.0's acc signal SH/single-fidelity/random search all choose wrong (the bottleneck is the signal, not the budget); ② **P5 wall-clock is untrustworthy** — the same configuration and the same code differ by 4× merely because of the measurement window, and the main framing was revised by the pre-registered clause to epochs-to-gate; ③ P3 the L0 hard constraint makes a real contribution to "selection"; ④ P4 causes collateral damage. Two rounds of signal iteration: **v1.1** prioritizes epochs-to-gate (regret 2→0 at the same budget; seeds 0-2 for selection, 3-7 for evaluation); **v1.2** adds a width FLOPs factor (blocks a 4× more expensive competitor). The champion is cfg1 (euclid, w16, lr1e-3) in all three versions. Applicability boundary: all conclusions are on the single grid64 task; P11/P12 belong to the pipeline / are not blind tests.

**Optimizer v2–v2.3 wrap-up + framing finalized (2026-09-23)**: since v2 the space adds the teacher-codebook K_t axis ({128,256,512,1024,2048}, 90 configurations in total). Third framing revision (final): wall-clock → raw epochs-to-gate → **normalized cost = epochs-to-gate × (conv(w) + K·d)**. As soon as the framing changes, the verdict flips: P2 goes from "not supported" to **supported — pruning has value** (SH regret 3.93 vs random search 15.82). **v2/v2.3 K_t axis (capacity greed)**: **Q1 is falsified** (the K_t=128 tier is feasible 9/18, the true structure is a codebook×metric interaction); **G1/R1 are falsified** (the K_t=2048 codebook gate 6.10 excludes no configuration, 18/18 feasible); **but greed limits itself to K_t=1024** (in-tier optimal normalized cost 128:7.0→256:6.1→512:4.2→**1024:3.4**→2048:5.0, bottoming out and turning back up), the mechanism = the gate "asks for more epochs" + the linear growth of the VQ term. Conclusion: the gate does not exclude configurations, it acts as a soft upper bound through the cost composition. Two further self-corrections recorded ((12) the missing cost term, overgeneralization from too narrow a range; (14) "H and the gate rise together" is falsified, the true regularity is H@acc-passes-gate ≈ constant 4.2–4.9). Project positioning narrowed: the "predicting cliff localizability" main line is exhausted and ruled dead, and the tool is positioned as a measurement tool + applicability-domain statement.

**v3.0 second-task generalization validation (2026-09-25, shape64)**: first hit a data-pipeline bug (`make_data` passed the seed positionally as `sigma_pix`, the first round's 53 cells were voided and deleted; the feature signature = failure level depends only on the seed → a pipeline diagnostic, not dynamics). After the fix, 216 cells: **T1 core supported** (v1.2 normalized signal regret=0 holds across tasks; contrast v1.1 raw epochs 41.25, v1.0 acc 2.95); **T2 supported** (the knee K_t=1024 reproduces across tasks, three-tier values bit-for-bit identical to grid64); **T4 not supported** (L_func@10 rank correlation \|r\|<0.3); **T3 partial** (the 256 anchor tier reproduces 18/18; the codebook×metric interaction is undecidable because the 128 tier was not collected); G1/G3/R1 reproduce in the same direction. Three harness revisions (the tier-limited replay pool follows the actually collected set, uncollected tiers are re-judged "undecidable", the T1–T4 report is landed). Methodological lesson: **when comparing "in-tier optimal cost" across tiers, the seed sets of the tiers must be identical**. Ground-floor self-check: an additional real-training ep20 vs prefix-replay comparison is consistent 3/3 (covering the "already passed the gate" branch).

**2026-09-30 campaign (CP-H / PA1 / PA4 / PA3 / MOE, toolkit v0.4.0)**: see cifar10-anchor.md and framework.md §10 — the two real-task axes, the A-001 corollary contrasts (P-A1-3/4, P-A3-1/2), two-tier capacity validation; the corresponding section of this archive collects the refuted → overturned records from it.
