## 1. PA1: the P-A1-3 verdict -- A (supports A-001, the counterexample does not hold)

**Question** (framework §8, highest priority): A-001's corollary -- if distinguishability is a function of capacity, then a "capacity sufficient but cannot spread out" case should not exist. The v3.0 K_t=128 layer is the suspected counterexample: ln128≈4.85 > task H(Y)≈4.16 nats (nominal capacity sufficient), yet the archived 80ep acc 0.83–0.90 does not pass the gate.

**Experiment**: the budget ladder (40/80/120/160/200ep, 3 seeds, grid64 CNN-euclid, unit construction bit-faithful to cp3_multilevel).

| seed | 40ep | 80ep | 120ep | 160ep | 200ep |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| s0 | 0.893 (H=4.25) | 0.885 (4.38) | **0.9995 (4.61)** | 0.9995 (4.63) | **1.0000 (4.32)** |
| s1 | 0.883 (4.24) | 0.996 (4.34) | 1.0000 (4.57) | 1.0000 (4.57) | 1.0000 (4.64) |
| s2 | 0.825 (4.30) | 0.998 (4.48) | 1.0000 (4.58) | 1.0000 (4.60) | 1.0000 (4.55) |

**Verdict: A -- slow reachability**. All three seeds meet the gate at 120ep and all are 1.0000 at 200ep. **K_t=128 is not a counterexample**: it is the "thin margin + high cost" predicted by A-002 (axis 3) -- the nominal channel is only 0.69 nats wider than the wall, so reachability becomes slower (~120ep vs ~40ep for healthy layers), but given the budget it eventually reaches 1.0. **A-001's corollary passes its most direct test.**

**Archive correction (the CPU rerun comparison has been done, 2026-09-30)**: research-summary 4.5's "K_t=128 is invalid in the end" is void. The CPU rerun (`pa1_demo.py --max-epochs 80`, device=cpu, separate fingerprint) gives three tiers of evidence:

1. **CPU 40ep and archived 40ep agree bit-for-bit** (0.8292/0.7250/0.8161, H 4.22/4.15/4.25) → data, initialization and the first-40-epoch code path share a source with the archive (the torch-version-difference hypothesis is **excluded** -- if the RNG sequences were incompatible, 40ep could not agree bit-for-bit);
2. **CPU 80ep (0.927/0.990/0.794) and archived 80ep (0.90/0.83/0.87) diverge** → the archived 80ep row is **not a continuation of that deterministic trajectory**; past epoch 40 it took a different path. The mechanism is undetermined (candidates: the rung-extension resume did not save the RNG state / a difference in the code's later behavior at the time; `cp3_multilevel.log` only holds "imports ok", so it cannot be pinned down) -- the archived "80ep plateau" is a **trajectory-bifurcation artifact**;
3. **GPU 40ep (0.893/0.883/0.825) ≠ CPU 40ep** → the CUDA RNG initialization differs from the CPU one, and the trajectories bifurcate from epoch 1 (original hypothesis (1) confirmed).

**Zero effect on the verdict**: among the three trajectories, the two independent ones -- CPU (s1 0.990@80ep) and GPU (all meet the gate at 120ep) -- both prove "reachable", so verdict A does not depend on the archived numbers. Another instance of "the negative result itself is wrong": this time it is a trajectory-bifurcation artifact.

**Incidental finding (descriptive)**: for s0, from 160→200ep, H(Z_t) goes 4.63→4.32 and util 98%→70% while acc climbs to 1.0 -- the later phase squeezes out non-task entropy (headroom 0.48→0.16). "The representation finally converges to task information + a small overhead" is exactly what the closure principle should look like.

## 2. CP-H axis 1: transfer of the distillation exclusion criterion to a real task

**Configuration**: CIFAR-10 + SmallCNN(width96), teacher K=256 / 120ep / cosine, student grid K ∈ {2…256}, 13 levels in total, 3 seeds. G0 gate 0.78 (recalibrated 2026-09-29; the justification chain for the K_min quantization is in the cph_demo docstring (5)/(8)).

### 2.1 Per-criterion results

| criterion | s0 | s1 | s2 | conclusion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| teacher acc / G0 | 0.799 **PASS** | 0.768 **FAIL** | 0.803 **PASS** | 2/3; s1 is 0.012 short, within one noise band, marked as-is, **not re-rolled** |
| K_min (Fano) | 4 | 4 | 4 | **bit-for-bit agreement** with the quantization prediction of the recalibrated gate |
| R1a (below the bound must fail) | PASS | PASS | PASS | **3/3: the exclusion criterion holds on a real task** |
| R1b (operational screening) | PASS | PASS | PASS | 3/3 (smallest gap 0.56–0.62) |
| R4 (rho(g, logK)) | −0.98 | −0.98 | −0.99 | **3/3: capacity dominates, extremely stable** |
| plateau established | no (Δ=0.025) | **yes** | no (Δ=0.038) | 1/3, all stuck at the edge of the 0.02 tolerance |
| K* (gap criterion) | not located | **128** | not located | the only locating seed gives **K*/K_min = 32×** |

### 2.2 Structural readings

- **The exclusion criterion transfers successfully**: students below the Fano bound (K=2/3/4) are pinned down on all three seeds (acc 0.10–0.21, gap ≥ 0.56) and are operationally screened out. **On a real task the tool's "can judge that it does not fit" half holds up** (the hard result viewpoint §7.1 wanted).
- **cliff location does not transfer**: the plateau is established for 1/3 seeds and the only K*=128 → **practical margin ≈ 32×K_min** (more than ten times the 1.7–3.0 of the synthetic tasks); the knee is diffuse over K=8–32 (logK 2.1–3.5 ≈ 1.5–2.5 times R_IB), followed by a slow-decay tail spanning two orders of magnitude. **The tail is the budget cost of the KD objective, not capacity**: the K=256 student (an architectural twin of the teacher) only reaches 0.752 vs teacher 0.799 -- CE+KL has not converged at equal budget; the plateau check just missed by 0.025 vs 0.02.
- **The archived conclusion is refined**: "10 classes, no wall ⇒ fails" (C3-CIFAR, 2026-09-19) → **"wall present (exclusion measurable), no cliff (location not measurable)"**. The weak-teacher artifact is excluded (this run's teacher 0.77–0.80 meets the bar), but the error in the no-wall conclusion lies not in the teacher's strength but in **the type of wall**: on a real task the capacity-accuracy mapping is diffuse, and the cliff is a structure exclusive to hard-wall tasks.
- **Gate discipline**: 0.78 stays untouched (no post-hoc change); seed 1 is archived RESTRICTED. **"Re-rolling to pass the gate" is explicitly refused** -- that is selection on noise.

## 3. CP-H axis 2: the first test of the closure principle outside the saturated regime

### 3.1 S1 premise: holds (non-saturated regime confirmed)

teacher median **err_1nn = 0.228** (grid64 is 0), **silhouette = 0.187** (≪ 0.6). The CIFAR-10 representation is indeed non-saturated -- **the closure principle is tested outside its original domain of applicability and the premise holds**.

### 3.2 S2/S3 (14 knee-window points: 5 points each for s0/s2 from {2..8} + 4 points for s1 from {64..256})

Within the window, rho(logK, acc) = **+0.90**; the capacity-proxy baselines are rho(H(Z), acc) = +0.93 and rho(H(Z), logK) = +0.97.

| reading | rho(acc) | rho(logK) | verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I_plug | +0.92 | +0.82 | carries quality information but is capacity-correlated (gray zone) |
| silhouette | +0.64 | +0.64 | gray zone |
| separation | +0.68 | +0.81 | gray zone |
| d′ | +0.60 | +0.80 | gray zone |

**Verdict: MIXED/gray zone** -- no independent quality reading (H_indep not established), and no pure capacity proxy either (H_proxy not confirmed).

### 3.3 Interpretation (archived as-is, separating "pre-registered verdict" from "post-hoc observation")

1. **S2 passing for all ×4 is a new fact**: in the non-saturated regime the distinguishability readings **carry quality information** (rho(acc) 0.60–0.92), in contrast with the "constant, no resolving power" of the synthetic saturated regime (CP3 supplementary test 1's 4.033±0.000). The closure principle's premise "no information in the sufficient regime" does not hold outside the domain -- this in itself precisely delimits the closure's applicability domain.
2. **H_indep is not established**: no reading is capacity-independent (all rho(logK) ≥ 0.64). Together with the within-window acc↔logK collinearity of 0.90, "the reading is independent of capacity" and "the reading only reflects capacity" are **structurally hard to separate** on 14 points (the pre-registered S3 dual threshold is precisely the gray zone set up for this).
3. **Arbitration observation (not pre-registered, marked post-hoc)**: i_plug's rho with acc, 0.92, ≈ a twin of acc, while the other three are weaker -- by project lesson 12 (a new metric must beat the cheapest existing reading), none of the four readings earns independent standing. This is in line with CP-D's J5 arbitration: **having resolving power ≠ being useful**.
4. **The net statement about A-001**: outside the domain the closure principle **survives in weak form** -- no independent distinguishability axis beyond capacity was found (H_indep not passed, consistent with all history), but the readings no longer degenerate into pure proxies either (H_proxy not passed). Distinguishability and capacity are **highly entangled** (0.64–0.82): neither the strong assertion (pure proxy) nor the strong counter-assertion (independent axis) is supported.

### 3.4 Post-hoc arbitration (cph_post zero-training post-analysis, descriptive, produces no verdict)

Cluster decomposition + partial correlation squeeze the gray zone one step further (`experiments/cph_post.py`, n=10/14, wide confidence intervals, **directional only**):

| reading | knee partial rho(acc\|logK) | bottom cluster (K≤8) rho(acc) | bottom-cluster partial | ruling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **H(Z) baseline (cheapest)** | +0.59 | **+0.82** | +0.58 | strongest marginally, partly through capacity |
| **i_plug** | **+0.71** | +0.78 | **+0.69** | the only reading with directional evidence "beyond capacity" |
| silhouette | +0.21 | +0.03 | +0.04 | **thoroughly dead** (the CP1 falsification reproduces across domains) |
| separation | −0.16 | +0.13 | −0.33 | none |
| d′ | −0.44 | −0.05 | −0.67 | none (even reversed, does not pass) |

(The four top-cluster points K≥64 are perfectly collinear; the partial correlations are all 0 by construction, no information.)

**Arbitration conclusion (by project lesson 12)**: i_plug is the only reading carrying directional "beyond capacity" information (partial correlation +0.69~0.71, only weakly better than the free H(Z) baseline's +0.58), but it **loses to H(Z) in the marginal arbitration** (0.82 ≥ 0.78), and by construction i_plug is an information-theoretic twin of acc (the Fano bound comes from acc). **The most honest statement: i_plug is at most "a tighter acc proxy than logK", not an independent second axis; the other three readings do not even have that qualification.** The gray zone thereby narrows to: **within the distinguishability-reading family there is no quality-check axis independent of capacity -- the weak form of the closure principle survives in the non-saturated regime, and it now has a quantitative statement.**

**Open-item update**: the partial-correlation test has now been done (post-hoc, descriptive) -- upgrading "i_plug's +0.69 vs H(Z)'s +0.58" into a pre-registered verdict would need more units (cost to be negotiated separately); the current directional conclusion neither supports nor falsifies an independent axis.

## 4b. PA4: the P-A1-4 dose-response verdict (2026-09-30, SUPPORT)

**Question** (analysis index P-A1-4): cliff sharpness is a function of output-mapping discreteness, not a property of capacity; the Fano exclusion is independent of discreteness.

**Experiment**: the grid64 image task with an added "output-mapping diffusion" knob (each sample, with probability δ, is blended 50/50 with a random other-class template), δ∈{0,0.15,0.3,0.5} × 3 seeds, the full teacher/student grid (10 levels in total after extending downward by {4,6}).

| dose δ | locatability | median sharpness | median err_1nn | K_min range | Q1 (R1a/R1b) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0.0 | 0.67 | 0.62 | 0.000 (discrete) | 35–64 | 3/3 PASS |
| 0.15 | 0.33 | 0.60 | 0.114 (diffuse) | 28–30 | 3/3 PASS |
| 0.3 | 0.33 | 0.53 | 0.223 (diffuse) | 13–14 | 3/3 PASS |
| 0.5 | 0.00 | 0.40 | 0.390 (diffuse) | 6–7 | 3/3 PASS |

**Verdict: SUPPORT (Q1–Q3 all pass)** -- (1) locatability and sharpness are monotonically non-increasing in δ, and the first-to-last locatability difference is 0.67 ≥ 1/3 (the intervention works); (2) the exclusion criterion passes 12/12, and K_min shifts down automatically with δ (35→6), so **the exclusion keeps holding as the wall position moves**; (3) err_1nn rises monotonically and cleanly crosses 0.02 → **the `DISCRETE_THRESHOLD=0.02` of `wall_geometry_probe` is formally calibrated** (0.000 at the δ=0 level, ≥0.114 at δ≥0.15).

**One protocol fix (accounted for as-is)**: the first round's δ=0.5 was misjudged as "refuted" -- at the time K_min=6-7 sat below the grid bottom K=8, so R1 had no below-bound grid point (a coverage defect), which was misread as a violation. After extending the grid downward by {4,6} and separating "undecidable (grid defect)" from "violation" in the criteria, Q1 passes 12/12. **A defect is not a scientific conclusion** -- at the mechanism level this was already visible in the first round (the K=8 student sits 0.55 below the teacher).

**Contribution to theory**: CP-H's "wall but no cliff" phenomenon obtains a **controlled reproduction and mechanistic attribution** on a synthetic task -- the sharpness of the cliff is determined by the discreteness of the output mapping; the applicability domain of the exclusion criterion is independent of discreteness. This also upgrades the CP-H axis-1 conclusion from "a single-task observation" to "a dose-response law" (P-A1-4 is SUPPORTed; the ledger can be filled in with the controlled result).
